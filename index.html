something I never say ♡

I had given you this website, but you never really looked at it. So I’ll use this as something where I can write all the things that maybe I’ll never be able to tell you or say to you in real life. I don’t know what I’m supposed to write or how I’m supposed to write it, but I guess I’ll just write here the things I can’t talk to you about. I don’t know how long I’ll keep doing this… but yeah.

Sometimes I want to tell you that you’re very indecisive. You can’t seem to decide for yourself what you actually want. Sometimes your behavior is one way, and sometimes it’s completely different. I know it’s not easy. None of this is ever easy. Neither stepping away from it is easy, nor carrying it along with you is easy. But maybe if you make one decision, things might become better.

I understand all of your behavior. Sometimes you talk to me properly, and sometimes you say that you’ll be busy for the next 15 days just because we’ve started talking a little too much and maybe need some space because we’ve gotten too used to talking to each other.

Sometimes a thought comes into my mind that maybe you don’t like me, and you just don’t want to explicitly say it and hurt me. Sometimes I feel like maybe all of this is more important to you than I am. I genuinely don’t understand what you feel. I want to come to you and ask you, talk to you, tell you everything, but maybe I can’t. Maybe I never will be able to.

This whole thing feels to me like… as long as I’m in your life right now, you don’t really want to let me out of it. But as soon as your university ends, or our daily-life interaction ends, maybe all of this will just disappear for you, and the value of all of this will disappear too.

one more thing ♡

I hate having these thoughts, but I don’t know… they feel like reality to me.

Just a day ago, you said, “We are not together.” I know we’re not, but I don’t know why something inside me broke when you said that. It hurt me in such a strange way. I don’t even know why, especially when I’m not holding on to any particular hopes anymore.

I don’t know, man…

I feel so bad when I can’t see you or when I can’t come and talk to you in real life. It’s really difficult for me. But I don’t want to make myself dependent on having you around in real life. I don’t want that. I really don’t.

Sometimes it feels so strange to carry the weight of all these thoughts and still somehow keep myself normal, keep smiling, go to university, and do everything like usual. But I have to. Because how long can anyone keep listening to someone’s problems or tolerate their sad mood?

The fact that you’ll probably never know about any of these things gives me some peace, but it also hurts me.

But in the end, you’ll only ever see me smiling. Ordinary yet not so special girl..
